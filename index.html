Marine Climate — Why It Matters

Ocean Health & Climate Science
71%Earth covered by ocean
90%Excess heat absorbed
50%Oxygen from oceans

The ocean is the beating heart of Earth's climate system. Covering over 71% of our planet's surface, it absorbs solar energy, regulates atmospheric temperature, and drives the global weather patterns that sustain every living organism on land and sea.

🌡️ Oceans have absorbed more than 90% of the excess heat trapped by greenhouse gases since the industrial era — quietly shielding us from even more extreme global warming while paying a devastating price in coral bleaching, sea-level rise, and deoxygenation.

Marine pollution directly accelerates climate vulnerability. Plastic waste, chemical runoff, oil spills, and excess nutrients create "dead zones" where oxygen levels drop too low to support life. There are now over 700 dead zones worldwide.

Ocean Science — The Big Picture

Oceanography & Marine Science
80%Ocean Unexplored
4Core Branches
11kmDeepest Point
1M+Unknown Species

Oceanography is the scientific study of the ocean — one of the most complex and least understood systems on Earth. It spans four core disciplines: physical, chemical, biological, and geological, each revealing a different dimension of the ocean's secrets.

🌊 Ocean biochemistry, biophysics, physiochemistry, and geography together explain how life thrives under extreme pressure and darkness — and how the ocean shapes our climate, weather, and the very air we breathe.

From the sunlit surface to the crushing hadal trenches, oceanographers use submersibles, satellites, autonomous floats, and sonar to map and understand a world that covers 71% of our planet yet remains mostly uncharted.
